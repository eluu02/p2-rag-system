# This month my payment is due on a Sunday. When is it really due?

This month my payment is due on a Sunday. When is it really due?

If your payment is due on a day on which the card issuer does not accept payments by mail, the card issuer must treat your payment as on time if it is received by mail by 5 p.m. (in the time zone where payments should be received by mail) on the next business day.

However, if you pay electronically or by phone you must do so by the day the payment is due.
