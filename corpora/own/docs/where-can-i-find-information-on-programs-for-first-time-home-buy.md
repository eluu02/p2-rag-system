# Where can I find information on programs for first-time home buyers?

Where can I find information on programs for first-time home buyers?

The U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD) has an online, nationwide list of agencies that offer resources and guidance on purchasing your first home. They also operate a phone hotline where you can get help.

Many states and local organizations offer programs to help first-time buyers with a down payment or closing costs. To find programs near you, look up a HUD-approved housing counseling agency in your area. Or call the HOPE™ Hotline, 24 hours a day, seven days a week, at (888) 995-HOPE (4673).
