# What is a wire transfer?

What is a wire transfer?

A wire transfer is a common way to electronically move money from one bank account to another. They can be domestic (between two U.S. accounts) or between a U.S. and an international account.

A domestic wire transfer is a way to transfer funds between two U.S. bank accounts or other financial services company. Wire transfers made by consumers from the United States to other countries made through a remittance transfer provider are "remittance transfers", and under federal law generally come with certain consumer protections.

If you have a problem with your wire transfer, contact the company right away and explain the issue. You can also submit a complaint to the CFPB.
