Is there any way I can check to see if the company or person I contact is permitted to make or broker mortgage loans?

Yes, the Nationwide Mortgage Licensing System & Registry (NMLS) maintains a free service where you can see if a financial-services company or professional is authorized to conduct business in your state.

Additionally, you can usually check if there has been an order of disciplinary action against the individual loan originator or company by checking with your state regulator .
