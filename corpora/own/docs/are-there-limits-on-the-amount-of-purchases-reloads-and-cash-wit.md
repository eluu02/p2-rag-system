# Are there limits on the amount of purchases, reloads, and cash withdrawals I can make with my prepaid card?

Are there limits on the amount of purchases, reloads, and cash withdrawals I can make with my prepaid card?

There may be limits on how much money you can have on your prepaid card, how much you can deposit, how much you can spend, and how much you can withdraw in cash at one time.

The limits on purchases, reloads, and cash withdrawals vary, so check your cardholder agreement. Your prepaid card provider is required to make this information available to you on its website or send to you upon request.
