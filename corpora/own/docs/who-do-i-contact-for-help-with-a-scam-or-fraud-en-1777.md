# Who do I contact for help with a scam or fraud?

If you think you or someone you know is a victim of fraud or a scam, contact the Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI), the Federal Trade Commission (FTC), your state attorney general, or your local police.

If you think you or someone you know is a victim of ascam, contacttheFederal Bureau of Investigation (FBI), the Federal Trade Commission (FTC),your state attorney general, oryour local police.

Report the fraud or scam to the FTC at reportfraud.ftc.gov or FBI Internet Crimes Complaint Center at ic3.gov.
