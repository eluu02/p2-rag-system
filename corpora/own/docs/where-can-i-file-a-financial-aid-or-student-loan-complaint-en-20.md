# Where can I file a financial aid or student loan complaint?

Where can I file a financial aid or student loan complaint?

If you’re having an issue with your college or university’s financial aid or student loan, you can file a complaint with a government agency.

Depending on the type of aid you receive for your schooling, you can submit a complaint to these government agencies, and they will work to get you a response.

Type of Aid | Government agency | Where to submit a complaint

Federal Financial Aid | Department of Education (ED) | ED’s Feedback Center
GI Bill benefits | Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) | VA’s Feedback System
Military Tuition Assistance (TA) or MyCAA benefits | Department of Defense (DoD) | DOD’s Postsecondary Education Complaint System
Private student loan | CFPB | Online or by calling us at (855) 411-CFPB (2372).
Servicing of a federal student loan | CFPB | Online or by calling us at (855) 411-CFPB (2372).

Depending on your issue, you may also want to file a complaint with your school’s accrediting agency or state approval agency. Note that if your school closed, there are processes for getting student loans discharged.
