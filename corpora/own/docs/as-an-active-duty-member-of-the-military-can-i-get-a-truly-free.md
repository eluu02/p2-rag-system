# As an active-duty member of the military, can I get a truly free credit score for me or my spouse?

As an active-duty member of the military, can I get a truly free credit score for me or my spouse?

Yes, as a servicemember or military spouse, you can get free credit scores and other helpful advice from your closest personal financial counselor or through Military OneSource.

For more information, find your nearest personal financial counselor or contact Military OneSource .
