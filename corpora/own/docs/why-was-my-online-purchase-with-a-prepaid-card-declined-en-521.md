# Why was my online purchase with a prepaid card declined?

Why was my online purchase with a prepaid card declined?

There are many reasons why a purchase could be declined including insufficient funds.

Prepaid cards can be used like a credit or debit card. And they can be declined for many reasons including:

You don’t have enough money left on your card.
You haven’t activated or registered your card.
The address you gave to make an online or phone purchase is different from the address you have on file with your prepaid card provider.
The provider does not have your zip code on file, so it wouldn’t match the zip code given at purchase.
The purchase was flagged as suspicious and triggered a fraud alert.
You have exceeded the card’s daily or monthly spending limits

Check your cardholder agreement or contact your card provider’s customer service number for more information or assistance.
